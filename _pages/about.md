# About Me

I am currently a Machine Learning Scientist at **Amazon Prime Video**. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [Stanford AI Lab](http://ai.stanford.edu), Stanford University, working under the guidance of [Dr. Stefano Ermon](https://cs.stanford.edu/~ermon/).

My research at Stanford focused on developing **efficient convolutional networks** optimized for run-time complexity, advancing **unsupervised and weakly supervised learning** methods to improve sample efficiency in downstream tasks, exploring **generative models**, and applying **machine learning to computational sustainability** challenges.

I earned my Ph.D. from the [Chester F. Carlson Center for Imaging Science](https://www.cis.rit.edu) at Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by [Dr. Matthew J. Hoffman](https://people.rit.edu/mjhsma/index.html).
